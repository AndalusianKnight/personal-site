Homepage lab · Nov 2026

Five front doors. Pick one, or mix them.

Each one is a real, working homepage built from the same site, terminal and content, so you can judge them properly. Open them on your laptop and on your phone, play with them, and tell me which you like. Mixing is a perfectly good answer.

  1. A

    Desktop

    The boot ends on a desktop. Drag the windows around, open rooms from the icons, empty the recycle bin, and find Pidan and Doufu asleep on the taskbar.

    Best at
    Paying off the boot. It feels like a computer you just switched on, and the terminal sits front and centre with a demo that types itself.
    Watch out for
    It's the busiest option. On a phone it becomes a home screen with a dock, which works but loses the fun of dragging windows.

    Open A →With the boot

  2. A-lite

    Lighter desktop

    Today's homepage, upgraded: the same big hero, plus a live terminal, a NOW.TXT panel and every room as a card with an invitation.

    Best at
    Being clear in five seconds. Anyone finds what they came for, and the terminal is impossible to miss.
    Watch out for
    It's the safest option, so it's also the least surprising. Closest to a normal personal site.

    Open A-lite →With the boot

  3. B

    Mission control

    A dashboard of your life: Shanghai time and what you're probably doing, a globe of your thirteen homes, the dogs' status, achievements and the next mission.

    Best at
    Showing a lot of personality at once, and it's alive: the clock ticks, the globe turns and Doufu does laps of her panel.
    Watch out for
    Nine panels compete for attention, and it gets long on a phone.

    Open B →With the boot

  4. C

    Terminal first

    The whole homepage is the terminal, with an ASCII portrait. Every command is a link, so nobody needs to know any.

    Best at
    The boldest and most memorable option, and the purest nod to the green-screen era.
    Watch out for
    Some visitors will hesitate before they realise they can click. The room links under the name are their way out.

    Open C →With the boot

  5. D

    Your room

    Your room in Shanghai, in pixel art. After the boot the camera pulls back from the computer on the desk, and everything that glows is a door.

    Best at
    Showing who you are without a word: the dogs, the pennant, the SG-1 poster, the DM screen. Day and night follow light and dark mode.
    Watch out for
    It's the furthest from a normal site and discovery takes a moment, so the plain list of doors under the room does the work for people in a hurry.

    Open D →With the boot

  6. Live now

    Today's homepage

    For comparison. It stays live until you pick, so nothing here changes the real site yet.

Easy to mix

  • D's room as the front door, with B's live Shanghai clock on the wall.
  • A-lite's clarity, with C's ASCII portrait as an easter egg behind the headshot.
  • A's sleeping dogs on the status bar of every page, whichever homepage wins.
  • B's NEXT MISSION panel, on any of them, for the people who came about work.

The same whichever you pick

  • The boot, and the short boot for returning visitors
  • The terminal on every page (press ~), now with commands you can click
  • The 90-second guided tour
  • Light and dark mode (the site starts dark, and remembers a visitor who picks light)
  • Every room, the achievements and the secrets
