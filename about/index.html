F1 · jessethe.com/about

Founder, product builder, forever a student.

I'm Jesse. I build things: two companies, six products, a D&D campaign that's been running for two years, and a home in thirteen cities so far. I grew up playing with my parents' old tech, which is why this site boots like one. When I'm not building, I'm 18 metres underwater, halfway up a boulder, or getting outsmarted by two rescue dogs from Huangshan.

I build products people can trust. I founded two startups, shipped six products from zero to market, and sold my second company, Tauria. Tauria made zero-knowledge encrypted messaging, file storage, calendars and video calls for regulated industries, so the company itself could never read its customers' data. That idea still shapes how I think about every product: earn the trust first, then earn the attention.

After the exit I went back to school and finished a Computer Science degree at Harvard, cum laude. History is my other great love, so I made it my secondary field, and I've kept taking courses ever since, from negotiation at HBS to Egyptian civilization.

I'm a bit of a nomad. I've called 13 cities home so far, from Toronto to Shanghai, where I am now. These days I travel with Player 2, my wife, and our two rescue dogs. Pidan and Doufu came into my life through volunteering with dog rescues in China, and the rescue work hasn't stopped since.

And yes, I love retro tech. You may have noticed.

NOW.TXT

After graduating I spent a year on the road with Player 2 and the dogs. Now I'm building personal projects and looking for my next big product challenge. If you're building something people need to trust, I'd love to hear about it.
