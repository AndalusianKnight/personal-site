I'm Jesse. I build things people can trust: two companies, six products and one exit so far. I grew up playing with my parents' old tech, which is why this site boots like one.

I've called thirteen cities home, from Toronto to Shanghai. These days I travel with Player 2 and two rescue dogs from Huangshan, who are in charge. On D&D nights I'm the dungeon master of Ionia, a Greco-Roman world my players have been lost in for years.

History is my other great love. I left university to build my first startup, and after selling Tauria I went back to finish, because I'd always wanted to: Computer Science at Harvard, with History as my secondary field. I've never stopped collecting courses.
