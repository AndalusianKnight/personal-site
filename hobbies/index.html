Off the clock.

When I'm not building, I'm 18 metres underwater, halfway up a boulder problem, or behind a dungeon master's screen. And there's a shelf of favourites I'll defend to anyone.

SHELF.EXE

The shelf

Books, shows and games I keep coming back to. Pick one up for my take. Some of them do something if you pick them up twice.

Books

  • Steve JobsWalter Isaacson · 2011

    The authorized biography, written from more than forty interviews with Jobs.

    Every founder should read it twice: once for the taste and the focus, once for the warnings. I kept the obsession with details nobody asked for.

  • MeditationsMarcus Aurelius · c. 170–180 AD

    A Roman emperor's private notes to himself on Stoic discipline.

    The most powerful man in the world, reminding himself every night to stay humble. Still the best management book I own.

  • The Three-Body ProblemLiu Cixin · 2008 · translated by Ken Liu, 2014

    Hard science fiction that starts in China's Cultural Revolution and turns into first contact.

    It opens in a piece of Chinese history most Western readers never learn, then goes somewhere much bigger. Read the whole trilogy.

  • Dungeon Master's GuideWizards of the Coast

    The rulebook every dungeon master keeps behind the screen.

    Half rulebook, half permission slip to make things up. Two years into my campaign, it's the most worn book I own.

Shows & films

  • Stargate SG-1MGM · 1997–2007 · 10 seasons

    A team steps through an ancient ring to a different planet every week.

    My favourite show, full stop. Ten seasons of mythology, history and a team that actually likes each other. Click the tape again to dial the gate.

  • Stargate AtlantisMGM · 2004–2009 · 5 seasons

    The expedition that found the lost city of the Ancients in the Pegasus galaxy.

    The spin-off that held up: a lost city, a new galaxy, and Rodney McKay's ego. Click again to dial Earth, all eight chevrons.

  • The Iron GiantWarner Bros. · 1999 · directed by Brad Bird

    A boy, a giant robot from space, and Cold War paranoia in 1957 Maine.

    "You are who you choose to be." The best Cold War film ever made for kids, and maybe for adults too.

  • ShrekDreamWorks · 2001

    An ogre, a donkey and every fairy tale turned inside out.

    Still funny on the tenth watch. A fairy tale that refuses to take itself seriously, which I respect.

Games

  • Halo 3Bungie · 2007 · Xbox 360

    Finish the fight. The end of the original trilogy.

    Couch co-op, late nights and the best ending of the trilogy. I still know the Warthog run by heart.

  • Civilization IVFiraxis · 2005 · PC

    Just one more turn. Its theme, "Baba Yetu", was the first video game music to win a Grammy.

    History as a strategy game, which is basically my two favourite things. "One more turn" has cost me more sleep than both startups.

  • Age of MythologyEnsemble Studios · 2002 · PC

    Real-time strategy with Greek, Egyptian and Norse gods on your side.

    A lifelong excuse to read mythology. My D&D campaign of Greek demigods owes it a debt.

  • Dungeons & DragonsTSR, then Wizards of the Coast · since 1974

    The game I run as dungeon master.

    Two years in, three Greek demigods are on a mythic quest across Ionia, and they still don't know what the oracle wants. Click the box again to roll the d20.
