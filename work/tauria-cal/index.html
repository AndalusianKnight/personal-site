Tauria Cal

Zero-knowledge calendar and scheduling, close to feature parity with Google Calendar.

Role
Founder, CEO & Head of Product
Platforms
Web, desktop, iOS, Android
Benchmark
Google Calendar
Outcome
Acquired with Tauria

Overview

A calendar says more than people think: who you meet, when, where and about what. Cal kept all of it encrypted, so a firm's schedule stayed as private as its files.

It had to work like the calendar everyone already knew, and it had to book a Calls meeting in one step.

Capabilities

  • Personal and team calendars, end-to-end encrypted
  • Invites, availability and scheduling across time zones
  • A Calls link on every meeting, one click to join
  • Reminders on web, desktop and mobile
  • Import from an existing calendar, so switching didn't mean starting over

Design decisions

  • Encrypt everything that tells a story: titles, notes, places and attendees, not just the description.
  • Scheduling with people outside Tauria had to be as easy as sending a link.
  • Time zones done right. A team spread across Toronto, The Hague, Belgrade and Lisbon tested that every single day.

Challenges

  • Showing someone when you're free without revealing what fills the busy time.
  • Inviting people who don't use Tauria, without putting the meeting details in plain text.
  • Recurring events across time zones: hard in any calendar, harder when the server can't read them.

Wins & losses

  • Close to feature parity with Google Calendar, under zero-knowledge encryption
  • Meetings booked in Cal opened straight into Calls
  • Calendar standards were built for plaintext, which turned every outside integration into a project
  • Built Cal as the glue between Connect and Calls
