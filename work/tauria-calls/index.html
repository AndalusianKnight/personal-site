Tauria Calls

Zero-knowledge encrypted video calls for regulated industries, built to stand next to Zoom feature for feature.

01The bet

Regulated teams needed video calls they could trust, and we bet the suite was the moat: start a meeting from a Connect thread, book it in Cal, share a file from Lockbox, all without leaving the encryption behind. No single-product rival could copy that.

02My part

I led product on the last piece of the suite, and on the admin security every customer's IT team asked for: geo-IP login monitoring, suspicious activity alerts, device and location blocklists, and an org-wide security dashboard.

03The hard call

Big meetings. End-to-end encryption costs the most when a call gets big, because every person who joins or leaves means new keys for everyone. We could chase Zoom's webinars, or keep Calls focused on the meetings that needed privacy most. We kept it focused: client calls, board meetings and case reviews.

REKEY.EXE

Someone joins. The keys change.

In an encrypted call every frame is locked with a key only the people on the call hold. When someone joins or leaves, every device gets a new key, so a newcomer can't decrypt what was said before they arrived and a leaver can't listen in after they've gone. The keys here are real AES-256, made in your browser.

04What happened

Calls completed the suite and brought it close to Zoom parity under zero-knowledge encryption. Across all four products we iterated our guardrails until account loss hit zero, and then Tauria was acquired.

  • 3,000encrypted calls a week
  • 4products in one suite
  • 0account-loss incidents

05What I'd do differently

Ship a smaller Calls sooner. I'd have launched calling inside Connect a year earlier and let customers tell us what a standalone app needed, instead of building the full app first.

Everything it could do
  • End-to-end encrypted meetings
  • Jump from a Connect thread straight into a call
  • Meetings scheduled from Tauria Cal
  • Share files from Lockbox during a call
  • Admin security: geo-IP login monitoring, suspicious activity alerts, device and location blocklists
The design rules we kept
  • The integrated suite is the moat. Calls, Connect, Lockbox and Cal hook into each other in ways single-product rivals can't copy.
  • Encryption is explained inside the product, with guided onboarding and in-app explainers.
  • Guardrails on every irreversible action, iterated until account loss reached zero.
