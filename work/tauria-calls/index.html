Tauria Calls

Zero-knowledge encrypted video calls for regulated industries, built to stand next to Zoom feature for feature.

Role
Founder, CEO & Head of Product
Platforms
Web, desktop, iOS, Android
Benchmark
Zoom
Outcome
Acquired with Tauria

Overview

Regulated teams needed video calls they could trust. Tauria Calls completed a four-product suite built so the company itself could never read customer data.

Calls was the last piece, and the one that tied the suite together: start a meeting from a Connect thread, book it in Cal, share a file from Lockbox, all without leaving the encryption behind.

Capabilities

  • End-to-end encrypted meetings
  • Jump from a Connect thread straight into a call
  • Meetings scheduled from Tauria Cal
  • Share files from Lockbox during a call
  • Admin security: geo-IP login monitoring, suspicious activity alerts, device and location blocklists

Design decisions

  • The integrated suite is the moat. Calls, Connect, Lockbox and Cal hook into each other in ways single-product rivals can't copy.
  • Encryption is explained inside the product, with guided onboarding and in-app explainers.
  • Guardrails on every irreversible action, iterated until account loss reached zero.

Challenges

  • Account recovery when the company never holds your keys: biometrics plus admin-assisted reset.
  • Encrypted search, retention and password changes with no standard playbook.
  • Learning what users need under strict privacy limits, with a privacy-constrained experimentation engine.

Wins & losses

  • Near Zoom parity under zero-knowledge encryption
  • Zero account-loss incidents
  • Acquired in 2023
  • Big meetings are where end-to-end encryption costs the most. We kept Calls focused on the meetings that needed privacy, not on webinars
  • Finished the suite with Calls instead of polishing the first three products forever
