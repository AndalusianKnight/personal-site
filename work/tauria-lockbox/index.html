Tauria Lockbox

Zero-knowledge file storage and sharing, close to feature parity with Dropbox.

Role
Founder, CEO & Head of Product
Platforms
Web, desktop, iOS, Android
Benchmark
Dropbox
Outcome
Acquired with Tauria

01The bet

Firms in regulated industries kept their most sensitive files (contracts, records, case files) in storage their vendor could open. We bet they'd move them to a Dropbox-grade app where nobody at Tauria could open a single file.

02My part

I owned the product: the sharing model, the guardrails on links that leave the company, and the call to build one storage layer for the whole suite.

03The hard call

Previews. Everyone expects a thumbnail before they open a file, and every storage service makes them on its servers. Ours weren't allowed to look inside. We could make previews on the server and quietly weaken the promise, or make them on the device after decryption and pay for it in engineering time and battery. We made them on the device. Nobody noticed the difference, which was the point.

04What happened
Close to feature parity with Dropbox under zero-knowledge encryption, and one storage layer behind every file in the suite: share it in Connect, present it in Calls, and it's the same encrypted file. The moment I remember: a client moving their whole archive into Lockbox the week a competitor's breach made the news.
  • 2.4Mfiles encrypted
  • 38 TBstored
  • 0files Tauria could open

    
05What I'd do differently
Treat sharing outside the company as its own product. That's where leaks happen, and we kept adding guardrails one at a time. I'd have designed external sharing from scratch, with its own onboarding, instead of growing it out of internal sharing.

    
Everything it could do
  • Encryption on your device before a file ever leaves it
  • Folders and sync across web, desktop and mobile
  • Secure sharing with teammates and outside clients
  • Version history, for when someone saves over the wrong file
  • Files attached in Connect threads and presented in Calls
The design rules we kept
  • Make the secure way the easy way. Encryption happens automatically, with no extra clicks.
  • Sharing outside the company is where leaks happen, so external shares got the most guardrails.
  • One storage layer for the whole suite, so a file shared in Connect is the same file in Lockbox.
