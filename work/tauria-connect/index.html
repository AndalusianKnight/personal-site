Tauria Connect

Zero-knowledge team messaging, close to feature parity with Slack.

Role
Founder, CEO & Head of Product
Platforms
Web, desktop, iOS, Android
Benchmark
Slack
Outcome
Acquired with Tauria

Overview

Regulated teams were doing their most sensitive work in chat apps their vendors could read. Connect gave them channels, threads and direct messages with the encryption done on their own devices, so Tauria's servers only ever stored ciphertext.

The bar was Slack. A secure tool nobody wants to use just pushes people back to the insecure one, so Connect had to feel as fast and familiar as the apps it replaced.

Capabilities

  • Channels, threads and direct messages, all end-to-end encrypted
  • Search across your messages, without the server ever reading them
  • Message retention policies for compliance teams
  • Files shared through Lockbox, and one click from a thread into a Calls meeting
  • Admin security: device management, login monitoring and suspicious activity alerts

Design decisions

  • Feel first. If Connect didn't feel like Slack, the encryption wouldn't matter, because nobody would use it.
  • Keys never leave the user's devices. Every feature was designed around that rule, never around exceptions to it.
  • Explain the encryption inside the product, in plain language, at the moment it matters.

Challenges

  • Encrypted search, message retention and password changes, with no standard playbook to follow.
  • Account recovery without ever holding the keys: biometrics and admin-assisted reset.
  • Bringing a whole team on board at once, when every new device needs keys of its own.

Wins & losses

  • Close to feature parity with Slack, under zero-knowledge encryption
  • Became the hub the rest of the suite plugged into
  • Search over encrypted data is never quite as instant as search over plaintext. We narrowed the gap, but never fully closed it
  • Built Connect first, so Lockbox, Cal and Calls had a home to plug into
