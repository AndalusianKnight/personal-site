Zebu · 2015–2018

Zebu

A modular ERP for small service businesses: the pivot that led to Tauria.

Role
Founder, CEO & Product Lead
Platforms
Mobile and desktop
Customers
Small service firms
Outcome
Pivoted into Tauria, which raised $5M

Overview

After Project Rebu, Zebu turned to small service businesses that were running on a pile of spreadsheets, a shared calendar, a chat app and a filing cabinet. Zebu put them on one simple system, built in modules, so a firm only switched on what it needed.

Building it showed us what those firms worried about most: where their clients' data lived, and who could read it. That question became Tauria.

Capabilities

  • Clients and jobs in one place, from the first call to the final invoice
  • Scheduling for teams out in the field
  • Quotes, invoices and payment tracking
  • Team messaging and files attached to each client
  • Modules a firm could switch on as it grew

Design decisions

  • Modular from day one. A three-person firm and a thirty-person firm shouldn't need the same software.
  • Mobile for the people doing the work, desktop for the office running it.
  • Plain language everywhere. No ERP jargon for people who had never bought an ERP.

Challenges

  • Small firms are busy, price-sensitive and loyal to their spreadsheets.
  • Every industry wanted its own workflow. Saying no to custom builds kept the product a product.
  • The messaging and file modules raised a question we couldn't ignore: who else can read this?

Wins & losses

  • Shipped a working modular product to real service firms
  • Found the problem that became Tauria: privacy for the tools teams talk and share in
  • A crowded market of all-in-one tools made growth slow
  • Pivoted again, this time into Tauria, which went on to raise $5M
