README.MD

How I build

  1. Earn trust first.Then earn attention. At Tauria that meant encryption the company itself couldn't break, and guardrails until account loss hit zero.
  2. Ship to real users, fast.Rebu carried real passengers before we knew if the market wanted it. Live rides teach more than surveys.
  3. Pivot without ego.Rebu became Zebu, and Zebu became Tauria. Each pivot kept what we learned and dropped what didn't work.
  4. Explain the hard part inside the product.Nobody reads the security whitepaper. Put the explanation where the decision happens.
