    ROAD_RULES.TXT

    How I move

    1. Move in, don't pass through.Stay long enough to have a regular café, a favourite street and neighbours who know the dogs by name.
    2. Find the coffee first.Every postcard on this page has my pick. The food pick comes straight after.
    3. Learn the words that matter.Hello, thank you, and "can the dog come in?" French got a workout in Montreal. Mandarin is getting one in Shanghai.
    4. Pack for four.Player 2, Pidan and Doufu come too. Every move starts with a vet visit and a pile of pet paperwork, which Pidan then tries to eat.
