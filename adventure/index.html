The Encrypted CV.

A five-minute text adventure across six of my homes. My CV is on an old floppy disk, encrypted, and the key was split into three parts, the way any good security founder would do it. Find all three and the printer does the rest.

How to play

  1. Type what you want to do, in plain words.Look, examine the whiteboard, take the floppy, talk to the duck, fly to Lisbon. Short words work best.
  2. On a phone? Tap instead.The buttons under the screen and the cities on the map type the common commands for you.
  3. Stuck? Type hint.It always knows where you are and what you're missing. No judgement.
  4. Your game saves itself.Close the tab and come back later. It's kept in this browser only. Type restart to begin again.
Walkthrough · spoilers
  1. In Toronto, read the whiteboard and take the floppy.
  2. Fly to Cambridge and talk to the duck. Key part 1.
  3. Fly to Lisbon, rent some gear and dive. Key part 2.
  4. Fly to Shenzhen, chase Doufu, then give the treats to Pidan. Key part 3.
  5. Fly home to Shanghai and use the floppy.

Amsterdam has nothing for the quest, but the train ride is lovely.

A small tribute to Colossal Cave Adventure and Zork, the games that taught a generation to type GO NORTH. Some of their magic words still work here.
