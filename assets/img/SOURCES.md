# Image library: where every picture came from

Images gathered for the site so they are easy to find in one place. Most are not used on a page yet: pick one, then point the page at its path.
Browse them all at `/lab/images/` on the site (unlinked, not indexed).

Downloaded on 2026-11-06. Rights in short:

- **Zebu and Tauria**: Jesse's own companies' marketing images and logos. Tauria's IP was acquired, so the new owner may hold the Tauria marks now; showing them as past work is normal portfolio use.
- **Covers and posters**: copyrighted by their publishers and studios. Show them small, to say which book, show or game it is (the usual "this is my shelf" use).
- **School, club and console logos**: trademarks of their owners. Fine for saying where you studied or what you played, not for anything that looks like an endorsement.
- **City and console photos from Wikimedia Commons**: free licences. CC BY and CC BY-SA photos need the credit line below wherever they appear (a small caption or a credits page is enough).
- **Flags**: public domain.
